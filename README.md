# CosmoPool

**▶ Play in your browser: [rblackwood224.github.io/CosmoPool](https://rblackwood224.github.io/CosmoPool/)**

A retro pixel-art pool game set among the stars, inspired by the 8-bit arcade pool games of the NES era. No install, no account. It runs in any modern browser on PC and on phones.

## About the game

Aim with a spinning star, push it further out for precise shots, watch the power meter pulse up and down, and press once to shoot at just the right moment. Pot a ball and you shoot again. Miss and it's your opponent's turn. If the cue ball drops, you lose a life.

The first table is a classic one. After that, every table is different: octagons, crosses, hourglasses, L-shapes, two rooms joined by a doorway… with 4 to 8 pockets, flipper-style bumpers (some of them moving), walls, wormholes and the occasional golden ball. The run goes on as long as your lives last.

## Features

- **1 player vs CPU** on Easy, Medium or Hard. The CPU sees walls and bumpers and even plays bank shots.
- **Couch co-op for 2 players**: each player presses a button on their own controller, the keyboard or the touch screen to join, and controls the game only with that device. Perfect for two gamepads in front of the TV.
- **Online 1v1**: share a 6-letter code or an invite link, then play with whatever you like (controller, keyboard, mouse or touch).
- **Endless practice mode**: just you, the tables and your lives.
- **Random tables**: every stage is generated and always solvable. Each one has a level code, so you can replay it or share it.
- **Combos**: pot several balls with one shot for DOUBLE!, TRIPLE! and bigger multipliers.
- **Blessing & curse cards** (optional): whoever falls behind on a table picks one of three cards. Drunk aim, black holes, magnet pockets, ghost cue ball and 30+ more. You can watch the CPU pick its card too.
- **The Point Goblin**: a sneaky little goblin who bags points from one player and hands them to the other.
- **The Goblin Duel**: on very rare occasions a goblin grabs the ball you were aiming at. Then comes a 5-second button-mashing tug of war. The shooter mashes to scare him away, the opponent mashes to help him push the ball far away.
- Smooth table-to-table transitions, neon title screen, animated galaxy background, chiptune-style sound effects
- **Top 10** with arcade-style 3-letter names and end-of-game stats
- **English & Hungarian**
- Music and sound effects have separate volume sliders

## Controls

| | Aim | Star closer / further | Shoot | Pause |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Keyboard** | ← → (Shift = fine) | ↑ ↓ | Space / Enter | Esc |
| **Mouse** | The star follows the mouse | (by distance) | Left click | Esc |
| **Touch** | Slide a finger anywhere | (by distance) | SHOT button | II button |
| **Controller** | D-pad / left stick (LB / RB = fine) | D-pad ↑ ↓ / right stick | A | Start |

On phones, turn the device sideways. The table then fills the screen and the scores move to the sides. On iPhone, use *Share → Add to Home Screen* to play full screen.

## Privacy

CosmoPool collects nothing. Settings and high scores are stored only in your own browser.
